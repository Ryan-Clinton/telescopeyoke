# The tube caught on the north leg: 11-12 November 2026

## Where

`./mount.py findhome`, 04:20 on 12 November, east of the pier, Dec +40°,
moving from hour angle -2 h to -1 h (the move log, cache/moved_log.jsonl:
"pointing survey, hour angle -1 h", 15° asked, 3.4° seen by the plate
solves). The back end of the tube (the camera, the filter wheel) caught on
the north tripod leg and the RA clutch slipped: the motor's counts went on,
the tube stopped. The person found the tube about half an inch too far back
in its rings: the long 183M train with the wheel hangs that much lower.

This is close to the meridian, not at the far hour angles the clearance
test of 2 November 2026 checked (6-7.5 h each side at Dec +40°, all
clear). Nothing in the limits keeps a GoTo out of this path; it was clear
until the tube sat further back.

## What the slip did before it was found

The handset's RA was about 50° out by 03:44 (three blind plate solves at
RA 1h33m, Dec +36.7, against the handset's 4.9 h; findhome measured +48.6
to +49.4° in hour angle at -4, -3 and -2 h, and +2.4-2.7° in Dec). The
plate solves agreed with the handset at 21:02 (the Soul, the probe), and a
run's GoTo at 22:54 already found the Soul 68' and 48' out; the pier flips
at about 23:00 and 02:10 are where a catch is most likely. The RA axis
counts went on agreeing with the handset, so neither noticed: only the
plate solves did. Every GoTo, limit and the night-end's park after it went
by counts about 50° from the truth.

## Afterwards

Home set again by eye and the handset reset at 04:22; its time set.
