Welcome to TelescopeYoke

This is what is ready and what still needs doing before a night at the telescope.

Camera

"Set up" checks the camera is plugged in and has a driver, takes Altair's library files out of their SDK if they are missing, and takes a test frame.

About

    

    
TelescopeYoke plans the night, slews a SynScan mount, plate-solves, helps you focus and stacks short exposures. Every move is checked against its limits and shown as a plan before anything turns.
